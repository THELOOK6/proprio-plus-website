Since the law adopted in 2019 (Bill 16), Quebec condo syndicates have more specific obligations for maintaining the building and funding major work. Two documents are at the heart of those obligations: the maintenance log and the contingency fund study.

## The maintenance log

It describes the building’s common areas, their condition, the work done and the work to come, with a maintenance schedule. It is prepared from a professional’s inspection and must be kept up to date as work is carried out.

## The contingency fund study

It estimates the cost of major repairs and replacements of the common areas over several years. It is used to set co-owners’ contributions to the contingency fund. It must be prepared by a professional and reviewed every five years.

## Why it matters

- Contributions that are too low lead, sooner or later, to unexpected special assessments.
- Buyers and lenders increasingly ask for these documents. A well-documented condo sells more easily.
- Planned maintenance generally costs less than emergency repairs.

## The dates that apply to your syndicate

A government regulation sets out the content of these documents and the deadlines for complying. The dates vary with the building’s situation: check the ones that apply to your syndicate, or ask us to do it.

## Where to start

1. Gather existing documents: plans, inspection reports, invoices for past work.
2. Hire a professional for the inspection and the contingency fund study.
3. Adjust the budget and contributions according to their recommendations.
4. Plan work according to the maintenance log’s schedule.
